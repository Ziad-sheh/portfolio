What I do

I lead regional creative for Apple across the UAE, KSA and the wider CEMEA region — copy and visual, from cultural insight and concept to the final shipped asset. Film, digital, social, print and OOH, across the tentpoles: Ramadan, Back to School, iPhone launches, and Apple Pay campaigns built for the region.

My work is bilingual by default. I rebuilt the Arabic creative approach from direct translation into culturally-native transcreation, and that became the team's standard. I hold the craft bar through review gates and shared systems — terminology, a copy database, a style library — so quality survives scale.

I lead a team of six copywriters, designers and art directors, and I build the tools we work with: three in-house AI systems now used agency-wide — an Arabic copy assistant, automated copy-QA and tracker automation — so the hours go into ideas instead of manual checks.
